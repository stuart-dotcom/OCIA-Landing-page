Inquire About OCIA

A Journey of Faith and Discovery

The Order of Christian Initiation of Adults (OCIA) formerly RCIA is a process designed for adults who, after hearing the mystery of Christ proclaimed, consciously and freely seek the living God.

This isn't just a class—it's a spiritual journey. We provide a welcoming, zero-pressure environment to ask questions, learn about Catholic teachings, and grow in a relationship with Christ.

  • No pressure: Inquiring does not commit you to becoming Catholic.
  • Open to all: Unbaptized, baptized Christians, and adult Catholics needing Confirmation.
  • Community-focused: Walk this journey with supportive sponsors and team members.
